# Free AI Freelancing Starter Guide

Educational material only. No earnings are guaranteed.

## Day 1 — Choose one narrow service
Try product descriptions, social captions, or content calendars. Define deliverables, turnaround, and revisions.

## Day 2 — Build three samples
Use fictional brands and clearly label each example as a practice sample. Never invent real clients or outcomes.

## Day 3 — Quality check
Verify facts, copyright, tone, client requirements, and privacy before delivering AI-assisted work.

## Day 4 — Make a one-page portfolio
Include a short bio, labeled samples, scope, turnaround, and contact information.

## Day 5 — Research prospects
Identify businesses that could genuinely benefit. Avoid unsolicited bulk messaging.

## Day 6 — Send a personalized proposal
“Hi, I noticed your product pages could use clearer descriptions. I can share a relevant sample and a fixed-scope proposal if helpful.”

## Day 7 — Measure and improve
Track meaningful conversations, replies, sample requests, and confirmed sales. Results are not guaranteed.

## Five useful prompts
1. Write five accurate captions for a [business] targeting [audience], with a [tone]. Avoid unsupported claims.
2. Rewrite this product description for clarity without inventing features: [text].
3. Plan seven days of content for [audience], specifying a goal for each post.
4. Audit this draft for factual accuracy, clarity, privacy, and copyright risks: [text].
5. Draft a short proposal for [service], including scope, delivery timeline, and two revision rounds.
